## What is Dialog Lab?

Dialog Lab pairs you one-on-one with someone who holds a different view on a topic you care about. An AI moderator stays in the chat to keep the conversation civil.

## Install the app

1. Open the [App Store]({{ site.appStoreUrl }}) on your iPhone.
2. Search for **Dialog Lab**, or follow the App Store link from [joindialoglab.com](https://joindialoglab.com).
3. Tap **Get**, then open Dialog Lab.
4. Allow notifications when prompted — you will need them to hear about matches.

## First-time setup

On first launch, walk through onboarding (a few minutes, once):

1. **Sign in** with Apple, Google, or email.
2. Create a **handle**, home state, and avatar (visible to partners).
3. Review the [Rules of Engagement](/support/moderation/), enable notifications, and accept the terms.

## Start a conversation

<ol class="beta-docs__steps">
  <li>
    <strong>Mark topics.</strong> In the Topics tab, thumbs up or down on issues you care about. You are only matched on topics you have marked.
    <a class="beta-docs__learn-more" href="/support/picking-topics/#how-to-mark-a-stance">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Get matched.</strong> From the Convos tab, tap <strong>Express Match</strong> — or start a search from a specific topic's detail page. Dialog Lab searches for a partner with an opposing view.
    <a class="beta-docs__learn-more" href="/support/starting-a-discussion/#get-matched">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Chat.</strong> Share why you hold your view, respond to your partner, and let the AI moderator keep things on track.
    <a class="beta-docs__learn-more" href="/support/moderation/#why-there-is-an-ai-moderator">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Reflect.</strong> When the conversation ends, leave feedback on how it went — including whether you felt heard.
    <a class="beta-docs__learn-more" href="/support/ending-a-discussion/#post-conversation-review">Learn more</a>
  </li>
</ol>

<div class="beta-docs__callout beta-docs__callout--info">
  <strong>Matching can take a while</strong>
  Finding a partner may take time when fewer people are online. Keep notifications on and leave matching running if you can.
</div>

## Troubleshooting

- **Cannot sign in** — Try Apple, Google, or email again. For email sign-in, check spam for the confirmation link or code.
- **No match notifications** — Enable notifications for Dialog Lab in iOS Settings. Without them, keep the app open while searching.
- **Bugs or crashes** — Screenshot the problem and email the team — or see [How to Get Support](/support/get-support/).
