## Pause vs end

From the chat menu (⋯), you have two options:

### Pause

Let your partner know you do not expect to reply for a chosen window — **1 to 24 hours**. This is a courtesy notification, not a lock on the conversation. **Both of you can keep chatting during a pause** if you want to. Use it when you are stepping away but do not want your partner to wonder why you went quiet.

{% docShot "chat-pause-sheet.webp", "Pause conversation sheet with duration options", "Pause for 1–24 hours" %}

### End

End the conversation permanently. You will be asked to confirm before the chat closes and neither of you can send more messages.

{% docShot "v1-end-chat-early.png", "End conversation confirmation", "Confirm ending" %}

If your partner ends the conversation, you will see that they ended it.

## Post-conversation review

After a conversation ends, Dialog Lab guides you through a short review:

1. **Did you feel heard?** — thumbs up or down on whether you felt heard in the conversation.
2. **Follow-up** — if you did not feel heard, note what went wrong; if you did, optionally send an **appreciation note** your partner will see.
3. **Private note** — keep personal reflections only you can see.

## Reporting from the review

If something went wrong during the conversation, you can **report your partner** from the review screen or from the chat menu during an active conversation. You will be asked to provide details about what happened. The team reviews reports along with the chat log.

{% docShot "chat-end-poor-experience-form.webp", "Form for reporting a poor conversation experience", "Report details" %}

Reporting is separate from the AI moderator's automatic warnings and blocks. See [Moderation](/support/moderation/) and [How to Get Support](/support/get-support/) for more.

## Finding past conversations

Completed conversations appear in the **Convos** tab. You can revisit the AI-generated summary and your review responses from there.
