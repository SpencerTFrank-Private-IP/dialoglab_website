## Profile tab overview

The **Profile** tab is your home for account settings and viewpoints. You may also see an **Invite a Friend** option.

{% docShot "profile-landing.webp", "Profile tab overview", "Profile home" %}

## Account Preferences

Under **Profile → Account Preferences**, you can manage:

### Visible to all users

- **Handle** — set during onboarding and currently locked after creation.
- **Home state** — shown to conversation partners.
- **Avatar** — icon and background color.

### Private

- **Email address** — never shown to other users. Depending on how you signed in, email may come from Apple, Google, or the address you used for email sign-in.
- **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** — legal documents.
- **Log Out** — sign out of your account on this device.
- **Delete Account** — permanently delete your Dialog Lab account and associated data. You will be asked to confirm before deletion proceeds.

## Viewpoints

**Profile → Viewpoints** shows every topic you have marked, split into:

- **Active Topics** — eligible for current matchmaking.
- **Expired Viewpoints** — topics no longer in rotation.

{% docShot "profile-viewpoints.webp", "Viewpoints list of active and expired topics", "Your viewpoints" %}

To add or change stances, vote on topics in the Topics tab.

## Invite a Friend

You may see **Invite a Friend** on the Profile tab. Use it to share an invite with someone you think would make a good conversation partner.
