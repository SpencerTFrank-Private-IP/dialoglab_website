## The Topics tab

After first-time setup, Dialog Lab opens on the **Topics** tab. Open it anytime later to browse issues available for conversation. Each topic represents a real-world question people disagree about.

Dialog Lab explains: *"Add a thumbs up or down… Any topics that you have marked could be chosen to discuss."*

## How to mark a stance

You will only be matched on topics where you have marked a stance. If you leave a topic unmarked, it will not be used for matchmaking.

- **Thumbs up** — you generally agree with or support the topic's viewpoint.
- **Thumbs down** — you generally disagree with or oppose it.
- **Tap again** — clears your stance if you change your mind.

You can vote from the Topics list or from the **Topic Detail Page**. If you have not marked any topics, you will be prompted to select some before you can get matched.

## Topic Detail Page

Tap a topic to open its detail screen. Depending on the topic, you may see:

- A short **AI Overview** of the issue.
- **News links** and related reading.
- A **hero image** when one is provided.
- Stance buttons (thumbs up / down) and a way to start matching on **this topic**.

## Share a topic

From the Topic Detail Page, use the share control (toolbar or **Share this Topic with a Friend**) to send a link via the system share sheet. Shared links look like `https://joindialoglab.com/topics/{id}`.

- Recipients who already have Dialog Lab open the topic in the app.
- Recipients without the app are directed to the App Store or the marketing site.

## Suggest a New Topic

On the Topics tab, tap **Suggest a New Topic** (or the lightbulb control) to propose an issue for the team to consider. Suggestions are reviewed by Dialog Lab staff — they are not published immediately. If a suggestion goes live, it may show who submitted it.

{% docShot "v1-suggest-topic.png", "Suggest a New Topic form", "Suggest a topic" %}

## Active and expired topics

Topics rotate over time. In **Profile → Viewpoints**, you can review:

- **Active Topics** — currently available for matching.
- **Expired Viewpoints** — topics you previously marked that are no longer in rotation.

{% docShot "profile-viewpoints.webp", "Profile Viewpoints showing active and expired topics", "Your viewpoints" %}

To add new active topics, return to the Topics tab and mark stances on issues you have not marked yet.

## While you wait for a match

If you are already searching for a partner, you can add more topics without cancelling your search. Open the topics sheet from the session lobby and mark additional stances — this can widen your matching pool. Starting a search from a single topic may replace a broader multi-topic search; the app will confirm if that happens.

## Review your viewpoints

Visit **Profile → Viewpoints** anytime to see every topic you have marked and update your stances. Keeping your viewpoints current helps Dialog Lab pair you on issues you actually care about.

## What is next?

Once you have marked at least one topic, you are ready for [Starting a Discussion](/support/starting-a-discussion/).
